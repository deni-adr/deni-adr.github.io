Uyunks

Uyunks is a personal tool that lets the account owner schedule and publish their own videos, mainly affiliate product content, to their own TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and grants the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.
  2. The owner chooses a video, writes the caption and picks the privacy setting.
  3. Uyunks publishes the video to the owner's own TikTok account through the Content Posting API.

Uyunks is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner to manage their own TikTok account.
