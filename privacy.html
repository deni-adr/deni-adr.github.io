Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Uyunks ("we", "the app") handles information when the account owner uses it with TikTok.

Information we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to post on the owner's behalf.
  • Videos and captions the owner chooses to upload.

How we use it

Information is used only to authenticate the owner and to upload and publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely and are never published. Videos are kept only as long as needed to complete posting.

Your choices

You can revoke Uyunks' access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. To request deletion of any stored data, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Third-party services

Uyunks uses TikTok's APIs, which are governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
